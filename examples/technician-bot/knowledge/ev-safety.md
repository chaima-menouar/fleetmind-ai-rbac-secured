# EV safety demo guide

This fictional sample is for software demonstrations only.

When a high-voltage or battery thermal warning is active, stop charging and
remove the vehicle from normal service. Record the alert code, state of charge,
ambient temperature, recent charging history, and any visible damage. A
qualified high-voltage technician must inspect the vehicle before it returns to
service.

Remote assistant output is advisory and must never replace the manufacturer's
official service information or a qualified technician.
